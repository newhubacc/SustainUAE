Sustainability in Numbers

Official statistics demonstrate Dubai's progress in clean energy, sustainable transportation and responsible waste management.

☀
Clean energy

2,860 MW

The Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Solar Park reached a production capacity of 2,860 MW, as reported by DEWA in September 2024.

Related goals: SDG 7 and SDG 13

Official source: DEWA, 2024 ↗
🚇
Sustainable transportation

747.1 million

Dubai recorded 747.1 million passenger journeys across public transport, shared mobility and taxis in 2024, an increase of 6.4% compared with 2023.

Related goals: SDG 11 and SDG 13

Official source: RTA, 2024 data ↗
♻
Waste management

1.9 million

Dubai Municipality reported in 2025 that the Warsan Waste-to-Energy Centre processes 1.9 million tonnes of waste annually and produces 220 MW of electricity.

Related goals: SDG 7, SDG 11 and SDG 12

Official source: Dubai Municipality, 2025 ↗

Figures represent the stated reporting periods and are not live measurements.

A balanced approach

The Three Pillars of Sustainability

Sustainable development requires a balance between environmental protection, economic prosperity and social well-being.

🌿

Environmental Sustainability

Protecting natural resources, reducing pollution, conserving biodiversity and addressing climate change to preserve the environment for future generations.

Example: Renewable energy projects and water conservation initiatives in Dubai.

📈

Economic Sustainability

Supporting long-term economic development through efficient resource use, responsible investment, innovation and green employment opportunities.

Example: Energy-efficient buildings can reduce electricity costs and support the green economy.

🤝

Social Sustainability

Promoting equality, public health, accessibility, education and community participation to improve quality of life for all residents.

Example: Accessible public transportation and community green spaces can improve social inclusion.

Source: United Nations 2030 Agenda ↗
