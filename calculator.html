    Understanding your results

    How Your Score Is Calculated

    The calculator uses a transparent educational scoring method based on the three pillars of sustainability.

    01

    Habit selection

    Each selected habit receives one point. Each unselected habit receives zero points. A maximum of 12 points is available.

    02

    Category scores

    Each sustainability category contains four habits. Its percentage is calculated by dividing the number of selected habits by four and multiplying by 100.

    Category score = selected habits ÷ 4 × 100

    03

    Overall Eco Score

    The overall percentage is calculated using all 12 habits. Each of the three sustainability categories contributes equally to the final result.

    Eco Score = selected habits ÷ 12 × 100

    What Does Your Score Mean?

    BEGINNER

    0% – 49%

    Room to improve

    You have opportunities to develop more sustainable habits. Start with simple changes such as reducing water waste and unnecessary electricity use.

    INTERMEDIATE

    50% – 79%

    Making progress

    You already practise several sustainable habits. Focus on improving your weaker sustainability categories and maintaining consistent behaviour.

    ADVANCED

    80% – 100%

    Eco Champion

    You demonstrate strong sustainability habits. Continue improving your lifestyle and encourage others to participate in sustainable community initiatives.

    These score ranges are educational categories created for EcoDubai. They are not internationally validated sustainability certification levels.

    Academic Methodology and Limitations

    The Eco Impact Calculator is an educational self-assessment tool developed for the SUST101 and SUST103 courses. It evaluates selected behaviours across the environmental, economic and social dimensions of sustainability.

    All habits are equally weighted to provide a simple and transparent assessment. In reality, different activities may have different levels of environmental, economic and social impact.

    The results depend on self-reported behaviour and do not account for household size, travel distance, electricity consumption or local infrastructure.

    Important: This calculator does not measure actual carbon emissions, financial savings or environmental performance. Its purpose is to encourage sustainability awareness, reflection and positive behavioural change.
